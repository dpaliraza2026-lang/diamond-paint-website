Who We Are

Built on Precision. Trusted for Decades.

Established in 1982, Diamond Paint Industries is Pakistan's premier coating manufacturer, delivering high-performance industrial and protective solutions from our state-of-the-art facility at Sundar Industrial Estate, Lahore.

Driven by rigorous research and global compliance, our operations hold prestigious international certifications from TUV Austria including ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, and ISO 45001:2018.
